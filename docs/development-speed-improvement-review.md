# Development speed: named actions

**Window:** v16 started **3 November 2026, 20:46:28.720763 UTC**; frozen **11 November 2026, 12:41:05 UTC**. [Top 30, full coverage matrix and evidence](development-speed-improvement-evidence.md).

The avoidable costs were missed continuation, unnecessary prerequisites, late contract failures and repeated coordination. Examples include a 7h15 continuation gap, a 13h16 image-inspection detour and 168 reads of one stalled diagnostic. Real software time and genuine decisions remain separate. The actions below are proposals; historical fixes do not establish that these practices are universally implemented. This revision changes documentation only.

1. **A01 — Resume the authorized stage.** Read the terminal result, identify the remaining normal stage and continue within existing authority. **Done when:** that stage starts, or one concrete missing decision is recorded; another status recap is insufficient.

2. **A02 — Budget phases and investigate stalls.** Include preparation, async work, reboot and cleanup in job deadlines; define a progress checkpoint before waiting. **Done when:** an actual result or bounded diagnosis replaces unchanged polling; preserve limits unless separately justified.

3. **A03 — Ask once with a decision packet.** Check prior authorization, then present exact remaining access, legal, shutdown or deletion scope and a prepared resolution. **Done when:** the decision is applied or remains explicitly pending; never reinterpret silence as approval.

4. **A04 — Check actual prerequisites once.** Verify selected trust, repositories, package ownership, capacity, DNS and guest connectivity; leave installer-owned preparation to its stage. **Done when:** prerequisites pass or the specific missing input is identified; remove redundant image inspection.

5. **A05 — Test installed-version contracts.** Exercise numeric types, literal templates, workflow links, delegation and ownership serialization with actual installed consumers. **Done when:** the focused regression and affected normal stage pass, preserving completed branches.

6. **A06 — Assign one CaC writer.** Name the owner of each firewall/interface field and reconcile additions, changes and removals through existing CaC. **Done when:** canonical/live readback agrees, unrelated guests and production protections remain intact, and no competing deployed store is needed.

7. **A07 — Clean up only proven remainders.** Inspect partial deletion or async completion before retrying; verify owned disks, temporary grants, credentials and final metadata. **Done when:** the authorized remainder is absent or restored correctly, without repeating completed destructive work.

8. **A08 — Bind selectors after allocation.** Take the completed normal allocation result and reconcile existing downstream selector fields plus their capture. **Done when:** readback identifies that exact reservation with credentials, prompts and limits preserved; never preassign a counter.

9. **A09 — Match tests to the change.** Select meaningful regressions, require a reason for another full suite and verify writable temporary paths before fixtures. **Done when:** necessary checks pass after the last relevant edit; documentation-only work gets document checks.

10. **A10 — Batch mandatory records and reads.** Update Beads, README, history, versions and recovery mirrors at meaningful checkpoints; reuse unchanged observations. **Done when:** required records are current and another read/export has a new fact or explicit validation purpose.

11. **A11 — Evaluate dependency reuse explicitly.** Compare consumed source revisions and dependency artifacts before proposing reuse. **Done when:** a controlled comparison proves freshness and measured benefit. The observed 177.364s repeat cost is not promised savings; no caching change is authorized here.

12. **A12 — Separate held and parallel work.** Keep withdrawn, held and deferred work out of the successor gate unless it is an actual prerequisite. **Done when:** authorized work continues without reminders or reactivation of unrelated lanes.

13. **A13 — Verify the requested behavior.** Use available tools and the agreed checks; distinguish HTTP protection from UI interaction. **Done when:** the requested behavior is proved, with explicit limits and waivers respected. A 401 plus Basic challenge can prove password protection.

14. **A14 — Choose an available transport first.** Compare the existing target-build/download paths and their prerequisites before introducing artifact caches or another key handoff. **Done when:** the chosen authorized path is demonstrated, or its genuine policy decision is isolated.

Retain identity/security checks, real approvals, cleanup proof and pre/post-reboot verification. At this freeze the host had rebooted; v18 allocation and post-reboot proof were not yet evidenced. Every ranked, repeated and additional finding is mapped to these actions in the appendix.
